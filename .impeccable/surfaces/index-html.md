# Grade 5 Chinese Learning Workbench

## Scope and visitor mode

Standalone interactive sample at `index.html`. Experience: learners enter an active lesson, manipulate the lesson machine, then try a short task.

## Audience and task

A Grade 5 learner in Hunan studies original first-semester Chinese language activities and plans a small daily practice step.

## Content and constraints

Eight broad learning themes with three original micro-lessons each. All explanations, examples, exercises, scripts, and illustrations are original. The route follows the unified Chinese textbook (People's Education Press edition) in a Hunan school context; it does not reproduce textbook passages, third-party lesson plans, illustrations, or video.

## Direction and memorable moment

The language-machine exhibit turns observation into understanding and expression. A moving lever and three labeled stages make the learning process visible; unit drawers, a practice slip, and a daily task list extend the same workshop grammar.
