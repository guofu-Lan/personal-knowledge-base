# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

The current surface serves a Grade 5 student in Hunan who is studying first-semester Chinese language arts.

## Product Purpose

This personal knowledge base organizes curated notes and linked source materials. This project adds an original, interactive learning sample to help a student understand ideas, practice them, and plan daily study.

## Positioning

The sample turns original explanations into a short animated lesson followed by a hands-on task and a practical study plan, rather than reproducing textbook passages or third-party lessons.

## Operating Context

The sample follows the unified Chinese-language textbook (People's Education Press edition) used for Grade 5, first semester. Hunan is the local school context; the textbook is not labeled as a Hunan-specific edition. A one-time school research schedule applies on Monday, November 16, 2026: Chinese 8:30–10:00, mathematics 10:30–12:00, then regular classes and normal dismissal.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- The first delivery is a complete demonstration for Grade 5 first-semester Chinese; broader grade coverage is a future direction, not part of this sample.
- All explanations, examples, exercises, scripts, animations, and illustrations in the sample must be original. Textbook titles and unit themes may be used for navigation; textbook passages and complete exercises must not be reproduced.
- If no video resource has verified permission, use original storyboard-based animated lessons instead.
- Keep personal notes outside this sample untouched.
- The user asked for the repository to be open source. The original sample uses the MIT License as a permissive licensing assumption; the repository's existing remote branches and history must be checked for unrelated private material before changing visibility.

## Evidence on Hand

The repository started with a README describing a personal knowledge base built from curated Markdown notes and linked source materials. The new learning sample is self-contained and uses inline original illustrations; no third-party lesson or media assets are required.

## Product Principles

- Teach through original explanations and examples.
- Make practice active, short, and understandable for a Grade 5 learner.
- Keep textbook references accurate and separate from Hunan's local school context.
- Distinguish temporary school notices from durable course information.
- Preserve the existing personal knowledge base while adding the requested sample.
